How igo works

City-focused · human in the loop
  1. Sign in with your email. We send a 6-digit code (or use 555555 while testing).
  2. Fill your profile and tick if you’re an owner, walker, or both.
  3. Owners post walks with date, time, city and max price.
  4. Walkers in the same city accept or bid. Owners can counter.
  5. Once a bid is accepted, the walker can’t take a new walk until this one is marked done.
  6. Owner & walker see the walk under “Current walks”, with ETA, last GPS ping and in-app chat.
