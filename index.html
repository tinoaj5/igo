How igo works

  1. Sign in with your email (we send a 6-digit code).
  2. Complete your profile and choose if you’re an owner, walker, or both.
  3. Owners post walks with date, time, and max price.
  4. Walkers in the same city get an email and can accept or bid.
  5. Owners accept, reject, or send a counter-offer.

For testing, the code 555555 always works instead of the email code.
