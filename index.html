How igo works

  1. Sign in with your email — we send a 6-digit code, no password.
  2. Choose your role: dog owner, walker, or both.
  3. Owners post walks with time, city, price, and dog info.
  4. Walkers in the same city see the walk and bid or accept.
  5. Once a bid is accepted, exact address & chat unlock for owner + walker.
  6. After the walk, the owner marks it as done. That’s it.

Why I built igo

This project started because organising dog walks in chats was messy: wrong times, wrong places, lost messages. I wanted something tiny, honest and practical — no big “platform”, just a clean way to match people who love dogs with people who need help.

Our values

  • Local first. You only see walks in your city, not spam from everywhere.
  • Privacy by default. Exact address and email are only used when a walk is accepted.
  • Clear rules. Time windows are strict: post within 4h of start, accept/counter up to 30 min before.
  • Honest money. Owners see bids clearly; walkers see counters clearly. No hidden fees here.
  • Dogs first. Everything is built around what is safest & clearest for the dog and the people.

igo does not provide insurance or take legal responsibility for the walk. It’s a tiny matching tool you can decide to use with people you trust.
