How igo works

  1. Sign in with your email (we send a 6-digit code).
  2. Complete your profile & choose if you’re an owner, walker, or both.
  3. Owners post walks with date, time, city, duration & max price.
  4. Walkers in the same city get notified and can accept or bid.
  5. Owner accepts / rejects / counters, then marks the walk as completed.
Dev mode Code 555555 always works.

Tip: Create one account with both roles, then open Owner tools to post a test walk and Walker tools to see how bidding feels.
