DogWalkr
Simple dog walking marketplace in Switzerland.

Post a walk. Let locals walk your dog.

Owners publish a walk request, walkers bid or accept instantly. No insurance — just a clean, simple matching tool.

📍 Switzerland only (for now) 🔐 OTP login (no password) 💬 Owners & walkers can be the same person

How it works

  1. Sign in with your email (we email a 6-digit code).
  2. Tick if you’re an owner, a walker, or both.
  3. Owners create jobs. Walkers in the same city get emails.
  4. Walkers bid or accept, owners can accept / reject / counter.

For testing, the code 555555 always works instead of the email code.
